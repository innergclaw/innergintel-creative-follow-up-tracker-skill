# Creative Follow-Up Stages

## Stage Definitions

| Stage | Meaning | Default next action |
|---|---|---|
| new_lead | Found but not contacted | Send first outreach |
| contacted | Outreach sent, no reply | Follow up |
| replied | Lead responded | Clarify need or send offer |
| proposal_needed | Interest is clear | Send package/proposal |
| invoice_needed | Buyer agreed or requested payment | Send invoice/payment link |
| paid_needs_info | Paid before full intake | Collect project details |
| active_project | Work has started | Confirm next milestone |
| follow_up | Waiting on answer/info/payment | Send specific follow-up |
| won | Paid and complete or active | Deliver/retain |
| lost | Not interested or dead lead | Stop outreach |

## Priority Rules

- High: paid, asked price, requested invoice, urgent deadline, replied positively.
- Medium: good business fit, visible creative gap, active social presence.
- Low: unclear fit, weak signal, no direct contact, low commercial need.

## Due Date Defaults

Use business days unless the user gives a different cadence.

- First follow-up after cold outreach: 2 business days.
- Warm reply: 1 business day.
- Proposal follow-up: 2 business days.
- Invoice follow-up: 1 business day.
- Paid missing intake: same day, then next day.

## Message Templates

### Cold Follow-Up

“Wanted to follow up on this. I noticed [specific issue] and can help with [specific fix]. Want me to send the quick package?”

### Warm Reply

“Got you. Based on what you said, the best fit is [offer]. It includes [deliverables]. Want me to send the payment link and intake?”

### Paid Missing Info

“I received the payment. Send me the brand name, deadline, colors/style, wording, and any reference links so I can move it into the queue.”

### Invoice Follow-Up

“Checking in on the invoice for [offer]. Once it’s paid, I’ll send the intake and start the next step.”
